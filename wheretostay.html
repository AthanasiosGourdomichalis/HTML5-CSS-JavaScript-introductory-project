Hotels & Places to Stay in Buenos Aires

Find the perfect place to stay during your visit to the vibrant city of Buenos Aires!

Top Hotels

  • Alvear Palace Hotel
    Address: Avenida Alvear 1891, Recoleta
    A luxurious 5-star hotel offering elegance, impeccable service, and a prime location in the Recoleta area.
  • Four Seasons Hotel Buenos Aires
    Address: Posadas 1086/88, Recoleta
    Combining modern amenities with historic charm, this hotel is known for its exceptional service and gourmet dining.
  • Faena Hotel Buenos Aires
    Address: Martha Salotti 445, Puerto Madero
    A unique design hotel in Puerto Madero offering a blend of luxury and artistic flair.

Budget-Friendly Options

  • Selina Palermo
    Address: Guatemala 4931, Palermo
    A modern hostel featuring budget-friendly rooms and affordable dormitories, located right in the heart of Palermo's vibrant nightlife.
  • Hotel Central Córdoba
    Address: San Martín 1021, Microcentro
    A budget hotel in the city center offering basic amenities and easy access to major attractions.

Unique Stays

  • CasaSur Bellini Hotel
    Address: Cabello 3780, Palermo
    A boutique hotel offering personalized service and stylish décor in the fashionable district of Palermo.
  • Duque Hotel Boutique & Spa
    Address: Guatemala 4364, Palermo Soho
    A cozy boutique hotel with a spa, ideal for a relaxing getaway.
