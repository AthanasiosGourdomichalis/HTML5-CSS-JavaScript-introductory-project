## Features
* **Home Portal** (`index.html`): Dynamic landing page featuring visual highlights, intuitive site navigation, and an immersive introduction to Argentina's capital.
* **Attractions & Barrios** (`attractions.html`): Curated showcase of iconic landmarks and historic neighborhoods (**La Boca**, **San Telmo**, **Recoleta**, **Puerto Madero**) organized in a responsive grid layout.
* **Αctivities & Culture** (`activities.html`): Comprehensive guide to city experiences, covering authentic tango shows, culinary tasting tours, and local architectural walks.
* **Historical Chronicle** (`history.html`): In-depth chronological timeline covering Mendoza’s founding, the British invasions, the Perón era, and economic milestones, accompanied by an integrated audio player featuring the Argentine national anthem.
* **Superclásico Derby Showcase**(`history.html`): Dedicated interactive tribute to the fierce Boca Juniors vs. River Plate rivalry, featuring dynamic DOM styling that shifts theme colors on user interaction.
* **Ιnteractive Trivia Quiz** (`quiz.html`): Vanilla JavaScript-powered quiz engine with real-time scoring, dynamic question iteration, and instant feedback on Buenos Aires trivia.
* **Visitor Contact** (`contact.html`): Dedicated inquiry portal featuring client-side form validation to verify user inputs before submission.
* **Travel & Transit Guide** (`howtotravel.html`): Practical transportation manual detailing international airport transfers (Ezeiza & Aeroparque), the Subte underground subway network, bus routes, and essential tips for navigating the city.
* **Accommodations & Stays** (`wheretostay.html`): Curated lodging directory broken down by neighborhood (Palermo, Recoleta, San Telmo), featuring handpicked boutique hotels, budget stays, and area safety overviews.
