# 🌍 Visit Buenos Aires! - Travel Guide Website 
## Introductory project to the Course "Introduction to Computer Science"
> ✳ Visit Buenos Aires is a travel guide-website for Argentina's capital, Buenos Aires!

## 🔗 **Live Demo** 
> You can explore the pages: Attractions, Activities, History, How to Travel, Where to stay, Quiz and Contact, through the menu, at the top of the page, where you will find detailed information about the sights, tourist activities, city history, flight travel info, hotels, a Trivia Quiz and our contact info, respectively.
> Finally, you can return to our homepage from any page,
by clicking the logo, at the top left of the menu!
