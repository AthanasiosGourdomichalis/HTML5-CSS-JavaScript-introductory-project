Obelisco de Buenos Aires
❮ ❯

Strongly influenced by European culture, Buenos Aires
is sometimes called the "Paris of the South"
or the "Paris of South America". It is one
of the most complex cities in Latin America,
known for its architecture, nightlife
and cultural events. It is also
one of the richest cities in Latin America,
with a large middle class and a large number of residents
with higher education.
